Frequently Asked Questions

Are table reservations required?

No reservations needed! Walk-ins are always warmly welcomed. If you plan to visit with a larger group (8+ guests), feel free to send us a quick note below so we can prepare comfortable seating for you.

Can I bring my pet?

Yes, we are pet-friendly! Furry companions on leashes are welcome in our shaded outdoor patio seating area.

Can I order ahead for pickup?

Absolutely. You can use our Order Page to customize your drink and pick it up fresh at the counter when you arrive.
