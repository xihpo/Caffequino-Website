About Caffequino

Caffequino is more than just a coffee shop; it is a small, family-run sanctuary that first opened its doors in 2015. What began as a shared passion for the perfect brew has grown into a beloved community space where every guest is treated like family. We believe that great coffee is the foundation of great conversations and even better memories.

We take immense pride in our process, roasting our own beans every single week to ensure peak freshness and a unique flavor profile you won't find anywhere else. From the careful selection of our single-origin beans to the precision of every manual pour-over, we brew every cup by hand with unwavering attention to detail.

Whether you crave the deep, complex notes of a dark roast or the bright, fruity acidity of a light roast, we have something special waiting for you. At Caffequino, every sip tells a story of passion, tradition, and the pursuit of excellence. Come home to the warmth of our craft.

Behind the Bar

Our Story & People

"Great coffee is an act of hospitality."

Founded in 2015 by coffee enthusiasts who wanted to bring a genuine craft-coffee culture to Cebu, Caffequino was born out of countless hours experimenting with roasts, brewing ratios, and creating a welcoming neighborhood haven.

Our head baristas and roasters treat every batch as a personal craft—training rigorously, tasting daily, and making sure that whether you're ordering a quick morning espresso or lingering over a cold brew, you experience the absolute best we have to offer.

Community & Sustainability

We believe that exceptional coffee should never come at the expense of our planet or farming communities. That's why we partner exclusively with growers who practice sustainable, ethical agriculture, ensuring fair wages and protecting local ecosystems.

Within our café, we prioritize eco-friendly packaging, energy-efficient small-batch roasting, and supporting local community initiatives. Every cup you enjoy at Caffequino contributes to a more sustainable, connected coffee future.
